TIME FLY

Earth through deep time, from 540 million years ago to the present.


DATA

The globe is baked from the datasets below. The baked frames are derived works; the
original files are not redistributed.

Topography and plate model, 540 million years ago to the present
  Scotese, C.R. & Wright, N. (2018). PALEOMAP Paleodigital Elevation Models
  (PaleoDEMS) for the Phanerozoic. PALEOMAP Project.
  https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.5460860
  - PaleoDEMs: the 1-degree netCDF maps (Maps 1-88) and the report
    "PALEOMAP PaleoDEMs" (Table 1 gives each map's age).
  - Plate model: PALEOMAP Plate Model (rotations and continental polygons), GPlates
    plate-model registry entry scotese_and_wright2018 (same Zenodo record).
  Licence: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0),
  https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/
  Changes: resampled onto a cube map, plate footprints rasterised and dilated, sea
  level and colours applied by Time Fly.

Ice sheets and recent topography, 26,000 years ago to the present
  ICE-6G_C (VM5a) glacial isostatic adjustment model, 1-degree files, W.R. Peltier,
  University of Toronto.
  Argus, D.F., Peltier, W.R., Drummond, R. & Moore, A.W. (2014). The Antarctica
  component of postglacial rebound model ICE-6G_C (VM5a) based on GPS positioning,
  exposure age dating of ice thicknesses, and relative sea level histories.
  Geophysical Journal International 198, 537-563.
  https://doi.org/10.1093/gji/ggu140
  Peltier, W.R., Argus, D.F. & Drummond, R. (2015). Space geodesy constrains ice age
  terminal deglaciation: the global ICE-6G_C (VM5a) model. Journal of Geophysical
  Research: Solid Earth 120, 450-487. https://doi.org/10.1002/2014JB011176
  Terms: the files state no licence and ask that both papers be cited. Distributed
  in baked form with the citations above, as approved by the project owner.

Global sea level, 798,000 years ago to the present
  Spratt, R.M. & Lisiecki, L.E. (2016). A Late Pleistocene sea level stack.
  Climate of the Past 12, 1079-1092. https://doi.org/10.5194/cp-12-1079-2016
  Dataset: NOAA National Centers for Environmental Information (NCEI), World Data
  Service for Paleoclimatology. https://doi.org/10.25921/rd66-5820
  (https://www.ncei.noaa.gov/access/paleo-search/study/19982)
  Terms: NOAA NCEI public data; cite the publication and the dataset DOI.


Terrain and bathymetry for region worlds
  ETOPO 2022 15 Arc-Second Global Relief Model, NOAA National Centers for
  Environmental Information (2022). https://doi.org/10.25921/fd45-gt74
  (the 60 arc-second product, surface elevation)
  Terms: NOAA NCEI public data; cite the dataset DOI.

Climate classes for region worlds
  Beck, H.E., McVicar, T.R., Vergopolan, N., Berg, A., Lutsko, N.J., Dufour, A.,
  Zeng, Z., Jiang, X., van Dijk, A.I.J.M. & Miralles, D.G. (2023). High-resolution
  (1 km) Koppen-Geiger maps for 1901-2099 based on constrained CMIP6 projections.
  Scientific Data 10, 724. Licence: CC BY 4.0.

Past climate for rivers, 120,000 years ago to the present
  Beyer, R.M., Krapp, M. & Manica, A. (2020). An empirical evaluation of bioclimatic
  and vegetation reconstructions for the last 120,000 years. Scientific Data 7, 236.
  https://doi.org/10.1038/s41597-020-0552-1
  Data: figshare, https://doi.org/10.6084/m9.figshare.12293345
  Licence: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0),
  https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/
  Changes: annual mean temperature (BIO1) and annual precipitation (BIO12) regridded
  to 1 degree.

Data notes
  Biomes in deep-time eras are zonal approximations, not reconstructions.
  Biomes in ice-age eras use the present-day climate.
  Mountain glaciers smaller than ICE-6G's 1-degree grid are approximated by a lowered
  snowline in glacial eras.

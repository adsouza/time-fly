# Art Pass Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Fix shaded snow and the coastal halo. Add painted, creeping sand dunes and stony
desert plains, real low-altitude swell and lapping swash with a riding foam line, and rock
banding where land is dry or bare.

**Architecture:**
- **Shaders:** all looks live in `terrain.wesl` and `water.wesl`, plus two small plain-WGSL
  modules (`planet.wesl`, `waves.wesl`). Each has a Rust mirror where a number must agree on CPU
  and GPU.
- **The wind is computed once per region on the CPU** (core `prevailing_wind_toward`, at the
  centre's latitude) and passed in the look uniforms. A region spans ±1.35° of latitude, so one
  direction per region is enough.
- **New per-biome weights** ride in the palette texture's unused alphas: sand in the ground
  texel, rock exposure in the rock texel. They blend like every other surface value.
- **Large patterns are keyed to a planet-fixed position.** That position comes from the region
  frame's exact azimuthal mapping.
- **Every periodic motion's period divides Bevy's 3600 s time wrap.**

**Tech Stack:** Bevy 0.20.1, WESL/WGSL, Rust 1.100, wgpu GPU parity tests (`tests/region_gpu.rs`).

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-23-art-pass-design.md`

## Global Constraints

- Bevy 0.20.1, WESL, the WebGPU baseline. Nothing beyond what M2 already uses.
- **Camera range:** from `CAMERA_CLEARANCE_M` (150 m) to `CAMERA_CEILING_M` (60 km) above the
  ground. Every look works across it.
- **No aliasing:** every new pattern fades out with `octave_weight(footprint, scale)` before it
  shrinks below a couple of pixels.
- **No new bake data.** Patterns depend only on position, time and data the shaders already
  have.
- **Large patterns use the planet-fixed position** (`planet.wesl`), not region-local metres:
  - the sand-sea mask;
  - the dune phase and warp;
  - the band warp.
- **Every time period divides 3600 s** (`TIME_WRAP_S`, Bevy's `Time<Real>` wrap that drives
  `globals.time`).
- **Anti-goals:**
  - no cracks, popping or swimming;
  - no shimmer from altitude;
  - no dunes outside deserts;
  - no banding under forest or snow;
  - at rest, the shoreline is still the height field's zero contour.
- **If the web budget is threatened, cut in the spec's order:** first the small dune octave,
  then wet sand, then swell displacement (keeping the normals).
- **Gates:**
  - `cargo test --workspace`, `cargo fmt --check`, native clippy `-D warnings`;
  - `cargo check -p timefly --target wasm32-unknown-unknown`;
  - the headless region bench (`tests/region_bench.rs`);
  - `cargo test --test region_gpu -- --ignored` on a GPU machine.
- **Repo rules:**
  - stage explicit paths only;
  - never run the windowed app with a timeout kill (`tools/dev/region-shots.sh` gives each run
    a fresh `TIMEFLY_LOG_DIR`);
  - keep free disk above 5 GB and `target/` under its 15 GB budget (target-guard cleans
    otherwise);
  - commit trailer `Co-Authored-By: Claude Opus 5.5 <noreply@anthropic.com>`.

## Review Focus

1. **Patterns from altitude.** Seen from 40 km, a sand sea or a banded scarp must show no
   moiré or crawling. *Owner:* Task 9's 40 km Sahara shot, plus a check that each new octave
   is wrapped in `octave_weight`.
2. **Near-flat shores.** At Beringia 21 ka the swash must reach no more than about 40 m.
   *Owner:* Task 6's `swash_amplitude_is_capped_by_slope` and a low Beringia shot.
3. **The hourly time wrap.** Dunes, swell and swash must not jump when `globals.time` wraps.
   *Owner:* Task 1's `periods_divide_the_time_wrap`.
4. **Poles and the antimeridian.** The wind and the planet position must stay continuous at
   |lat| near 90° and across ±180°. *Owner:* Task 3's continuity test and its parity cases at
   89° and at lon −179.9°.
5. **The Barren era and ice.** At 540 Ma bands show on all bare rock; IceCap gets no dunes and
   no bands. *Owner:* Task 2's palette-weight tests.

### Task 3: Prevailing wind and the planet-fixed position (Rust, WESL, parity)

**Why a fixed axis:** a crest coordinate taken along each pixel's own tangent axes,
`dot(p, east_px)`, is identically zero, because the tangent is perpendicular to `p`. So the dune
phase uses one 3D wind axis per region, `W = east_c·w.e + north_c·w.n` at the centre, as
`dot(p, W)`.
- The sand-sea mask, the warp and the band warp are exact functions of `p`.
- The crest phase can shift slightly between visits entered from different centres. That's
  accepted: dunes drift anyway.

**Files:**
- Create: `crates/timefly_core/src/worldgen/wind.rs` (`pub mod wind;` in `worldgen/mod.rs`)
- Create: `crates/timefly/assets/shaders/planet.wesl`. It is plain WGSL with no imports, like
  `snowline.wesl`.
- Modify: `crates/timefly/src/terrain/material.rs`:
  - a new shader handle `PLANET_SHADER`, with UUID `…9e16`, registered like
    `SNOWLINE_SHADER`;
  - `TerrainLook` and `terrain_look`.
- Modify: `crates/timefly/src/terrain/mod.rs:71` and `material.rs:85` (callers)
- Test: `crates/timefly/tests/region_gpu.rs` (one parity test)

**Interfaces:**
- Produces, in core: `pub fn prevailing_wind_toward(lat_deg: f64) -> DVec2`.
  - It returns the unit (east, north) direction the wind blows toward, from a bearing `b`
    (degrees clockwise from north) as `(sin b, cos b)`.
  - The zones:

    | Zone          | Northern hemisphere | Southern hemisphere |
    | ------------- | ------------------- | ------------------- |
    | Trades        | 225 (0–30°N)        | 315 (0–30°S)        |
    | Westerlies    | 70 (30–60°N)        | 110 (30–60°S)       |
    | Polar winds   | 250 (beyond 60°N)   | 290 (beyond 60°S)   |

  - Transitions blend the bearing linearly with `smoothstep` over ±5° around 0°, ±30° and ±60°.
    At the equator the bearing is `mix(315, 225, smoothstep(-5, 5, lat))`, which gives 270.
- Produces, in core: `pub fn wind_axis(frame: &RegionFrame) -> (DVec2, DVec3)`.
  - The first value is the region-local wind in the xz plane: `(w.e, -w.n)`, since `-z` is north.
  - The second is the 3D axis `W` above.
  - The latitude is the centre direction's era-frame latitude.
- Produces, in `planet.wesl`:
  `fn planet_position_m(xz: vec2<f32>, center: vec3<f32>, north: vec3<f32>, east: vec3<f32>, radius_m: f32) -> vec3<f32>`.
  - It is `radius_m * FrameBasis::local_to_dir(x, z)`: `offset = east*x - north*z`, and
    `angle = |offset| / R`.
- Produces: `TerrainLook` gains:
  - `frame_center: Vec4` (xyz the centre's unit direction, w `EARTH_RADIUS_M`);
  - `frame_north: Vec4` and `frame_east: Vec4` (xyz; w 0);
  - `wind: Vec4` (xyz `W`, w unused).
  - `terrain_look` gains a `frame: &RegionFrame` argument.
  - `TerrainLook::default()` uses the frame at render +Z.

### Task 5: Sand dunes and stony plain

**Files:**
- Modify: `material.rs`:

  - `DUNE_SMALL_SPACING_M: f32 = 200.0`;
  - `DUNE_PERIOD_S: f32 = 3600.0`: one main spacing per hour, 17 m/min;
  - `DUNE_SMALL_PERIOD_S: f32 = 600.0`;
  - `SAND_SEA_SCALE_M: f32 = 40_000.0`;
  - packed in a new `TerrainLook.dunes: Vec4` (spacing, small spacing, period, small period)
    and a new `TerrainLook.patterns: Vec4`, whose x is `SAND_SEA_SCALE_M` (Task 7 fills yzw).
- Modify: `terrain.wesl` (imports `package::planet`).

**What the shader does** (choices left open are the builder's, within the spec's §5.3 targets):
- **Desert weight:** `surf.sand`, times a sand-sea mask from planet-position noise at
  `SAND_SEA_SCALE_M`. The threshold puts about 25–33 % of desert ground in sand seas; record
  the measured share from the Sahara before/after pair.
  - Dunes are masked off by `rock`, `snow` and `beach`.
- **Crests:** perpendicular to the wind. The coordinate along the wind is
  `u = dot(p, look.wind.xyz) / spacing + warp(p) - t / period`, where `p` is
  `planet_position_m(xz, ..)` (Task 3).
  - The profile is asymmetric: windward to lee is 4:1, the lee steeper.
  - It gives `dune_gradient`, which is added to `shading` before `world_normal`, together
    with a lighter-crest, darker-trough albedo change.
- **Octaves:** two (main and small), each faded with `octave_weight`.
- **Phase:** only the phase moves; the warp noise is static, so motion is exactly periodic.
- **Stony plain:** desert ground outside sand seas gets darker gravel patches and two-scale
  mottling from planet-position noise.

### Task 6: Swell and swash

**Files:**
- Create: `crates/timefly/assets/shaders/waves.wesl` (plain WGSL, imported by `water.wesl` and,
  in Task 8, `terrain.wesl`)
- Modify: `water.rs`:
  - `WATER_RING_M` 25 → 12 (+27 rings, about 7k vertices). This is a static mesh change, not a
    camera-height-adaptive mesh. With the shortest displaced wavelength at 100 m, it gives at
    least 4 vertices per wavelength out to about 1 km (`swell_is_resolved_where_displaced`),
    which is what the spec's "finer when flying low" needs, at a fixed ~7k-vertex cost.
    Displacement fades out above 1 km. Ruling recorded here (2026-11-23).
  - `pub const SWELL_PERIODS_S: [f32; 3] = [8.0, 9.0, 10.0]`. Wavelengths follow deep-water
    dispersion, `λ = g·T²/2π`: 100, 126 and 156 m.
  - `SWELL_AMPLITUDE_M: f32 = 0.5` (per component, so at most 1.5 m);
  - `SWELL_FADE_AGL_M: [f32; 2] = [500.0, 1000.0]` (full below, none above);
  - `SWASH_PERIOD_S: f32 = 8.0`;
  - `SWASH_REACH_M: f32 = 25.0`;
  - `SWASH_MAX_AMPLITUDE_M: f32 = 0.5`;
  - `WaterLook` gains `swell: Vec4` and `swash: Vec4` with these, and `WaterLook.wind: Vec4`:
    xy is Task 3's region-local wind; zw are unused.
  - Add `pub fn swash_amplitude_m(floor_slope: f32) -> f32` = `min(SWASH_REACH_M * slope, SWASH_MAX_AMPLITUDE_M)`.
    `waves.wesl` mirrors it.
- Modify: `water.wesl`:
  - **Vertex stage:** adds the swell height. The swell is three directional components; their
    directions spread ±25° around `look.wind.xy` (the builder chooses the spread).
    - It is faded by depth (none where depth < 2 m, full beyond 10 m).
    - It is faded by camera height between the two AGLs.
    - It is faded by the local vertex spacing, `max(ring spacing, 2πr/WATER_SEGMENTS)`, so
      each wavelength has at least 4 vertices.
  - **Fragment stage:**
    - normals are computed analytically from the same function, and stay at all heights.
      Add larger, slow normal-only wave bands (400–1500 m) so moving light shows from high up.
    - the land test becomes `depth + swash_level <= 0`. The swash level is
      `swash_amplitude_m(floor slope) * sin(2π t / SWASH_PERIOD_S + phase(xz))`, where the
      phase is slow region-local noise and the floor slope is a central difference of
      `blended_height` over one inner texel.
    - It applies only where depth < 3 m.
    - A foam line rides the moving edge.

### Task 7: Rock banding

**Files:**
- Modify: `material.rs`:
  - `pub const BAND_SCALE_M: f32 = 35.0` (mean band thickness; the builder mixes 10–60 m);
  - `BAND_WARP_M: f32 = 40.0`;

- Modify: `terrain.wesl`.

**What the shader does:**
- **Weight:** `surf.exposure`, times one of:
  - `rock` on cliffs;
  - for exposure ≥ 0.8, `smoothstep(BAND_MIN_SLOPE - 0.05, BAND_MIN_SLOPE + 0.05, slope)` on
    moderate slopes.
- **Masked off by** snow and canopy.
- **Bands:** a function of `height + warp`, where `warp` is planet-position noise at km scales
  with amplitude `BAND_WARP_M`, so the layers dip and fold.
  - Each band varies `surf.rock` in value (±20 %) and in warm/cool hue (a few %).
  - Faded with `octave_weight(footprint, BAND_SCALE_M)`, using the band's vertical size across
    the pixel (slope times footprint).
